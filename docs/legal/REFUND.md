## 2. Refund terms for future paid services (pre-agreed, not yet operative)

When paid offerings exist, the following will apply unless superseded by the
then-current terms of sale:

- **Annual subscriptions**: written cancellation request by email within 14
  days of the initial purchase → full refund of that purchase. After 14 days
  → no pro-rata refund, but service continues to the end of the paid term.
- **Monthly subscriptions / usage-based charges**: refund at the operator's
  discretion for demonstrable service failure (e.g. the hosted Service
  unavailable for a materially extended period).
- **One-time services** (support retainers, custom parser work): refundable
  pro-rata for work not yet started; non-refundable once delivery has begun.
- Refunds are processed to the original payment method within 14 days of
  approval, subject to bank/processor timing.
