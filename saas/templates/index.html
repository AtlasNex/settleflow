SettleFlow

{# Audience named, fear answered, licence stated. The previous sub-head described the mechanism ("reconcile a settlement file against a bank statement") which is the builder's framing, not the buyer's problem. #}

For merchants and accountants reconciling Razorpay payouts against bank credits. Upload your settlement file and your bank CSV — get matched transactions, an exception list, and Tally / GST / TDS code-1035 workpapers.

Open source (MIT). Your files are used for this one reconciliation only; the run record is deleted after 30 days. No account, no card.

We detect the UTR, amount and date columns automatically. If your statement uses unusual headers, name them below.

Advanced — name the bank columns yourself

Leave blank to auto-detect. Column names are case-insensitive and must match your CSV header exactly otherwise.

How the matching works

  • It does not match on the UTR. Your bank statement carries the correspondent bank's UTR, which is a different number from the gateway's settlement_utr. The match key is the settlement id, with a (date + net amount) fallback.
  • Amounts are Decimal, in rupees. Gateway exports quote paise; the conversion happens once, at parse time.
  • Every match is inspectable. You get the matched ledger and the exception list, not a score.

Your data

Nothing is kept beyond the run itself: the uploaded files are written to a private temporary file, used, and deleted immediately. The run record (matched ledger, exceptions, generated CSVs) is deleted after 30 days, automatically. Privacy has the detail.

Currently supported: Razorpay settlement exports, and bank statements from HDFC, SBI, ICICI, Axis, Kotak, PNB and DBS (plus SBI PDFs). Cashfree, PhonePe, Juspay and PayU are not wired yet — see About.
